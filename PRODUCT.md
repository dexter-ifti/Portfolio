# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, collaborators, and other developers who want to quickly understand Taha's work, technical focus, resume, and public profiles.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Taha as a full-stack web developer, surfaces selected projects and skills, and creates clear paths to contact details, social profiles, and the resume.

## Brand Personality

Compact, technical, direct. The interface should feel like a personal developer space: concise, low-friction, and focused on proof of work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page structure, oversized hero marketing language, decorative card-heavy layouts, and visual elements that compete with the portfolio content.

## Design Principles

- Keep navigation and scanning friction low.
- Let projects, skills, and profile links carry the proof.
- Preserve the compact dark interface already established in the site.
- Prefer practical affordances over decorative flourishes.
- Make every route feel connected to the same personal identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible navigation, descriptive link labels, and reduced reliance on motion for comprehension.
